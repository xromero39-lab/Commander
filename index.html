CommandForge Help

Card size: Use the minus and plus buttons for quick resizing. Compact lets you browse many cards; Huge is for inspecting cards.

Focus Library: Expands the card library across the work area, temporarily hiding deck, side zone, and token tools. Click it again to return.

Search: Use names such as Sol Ring or Scryfall syntax like t:elf, o:draw, and id:ug.

Build: Drag library cards into Main Deck or Side Zone. Drag deck rows between the two zones. The buttons are fallbacks.

Tokens: Upload an image, give it a name, types, stats, and rules text, then drag it into a deck zone.
